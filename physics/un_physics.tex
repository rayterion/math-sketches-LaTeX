\section{1.5 Uncertainty and Significant Figures}

The density of a material is
equal to its mass divided by its volume. What is the density (in $kg/m^3$) of a rock of mass $1.80kg$ and volume $6.0 \times 10^{-4} m^3$?

\textbf{Solution:} Since the mass has three significant figures and the volume has only one, the smallest remains, so the answer should contain one significant figure.
\begin{equation}
    \frac{1.80}{6.0 \times 10^{-4}} \cdot \frac{kg}{m^3} = 3.0 \times 10^3
\end{equation}
